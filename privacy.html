Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

Gotop Technologies Limited ("we") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use gotapi.ai and our API gateway services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Name, email, company name (if applicable), and billing information (processed by Stripe — we do not store card details).

Usage Data: API request logs (timestamps, endpoints, status, latency), IP addresses, usage metrics (tokens processed), and error logs.

Website Analytics: Browser type, OS, referring URLs, and pages visited.

Communications: Information you provide when contacting us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Providing services, improving performance, security/fraud prevention, communication (updates, billing), and legal compliance.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect: your end users' personal data, content of API requests beyond routing needs, sensitive personal information, or payment card details.

4. Data Storage & Transfer

Location: United States (New York, DigitalOcean).

Retention: Account data: account duration + 90 days. Usage logs: up to 12 months. Billing records: 7 years (HK law).

International Transfers: Data may be processed in the US with appropriate safeguards.

5. Data Sharing

We share with payment processors (Stripe), cloud providers, and analytics providers (anonymized). We do NOT sell your data. We may disclose data if required by law.

6. Data Security

TLS 1.2+ encryption, encryption at rest, access controls, regular audits, and API key authentication.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data. Contact: privacy@gotapi.ai.

8. GDPR (European Users)

Legal basis: contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligations. DPO: dpo@gotapi.ai. You may lodge a complaint with your local authority.

9. Cookies

Essential cookies for site functionality. Analytics cookies (opt-in). No advertising or tracking cookies.

账户信息：姓名、邮箱、公司名称（如适用）、账单信息（由 Stripe 处理，我们不存储银行卡信息）。

2. 信息使用方式

提供服务、优化性能、安全防护、通信通知（更新、账单）及法律合规。

我们不收集：您的终端用户的个人数据、API 请求内容（超出路由所需）、敏感个人信息、支付卡信息。
