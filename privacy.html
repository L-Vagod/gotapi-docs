Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

Gotop Technologies Limited ("Company", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website gotapi.ai (the "Site") and our API gateway services (the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you register for an account, we collect: name and email address, company name (if applicable), billing information (processed by our payment processor — we do not store credit card numbers), and account credentials.

1.2 Usage Data

When you use our API Services, we automatically collect: API request logs (timestamps, endpoint called, response status, latency), IP addresses of API requests (for rate limiting and abuse prevention), usage metrics (number of requests, tokens processed), and error logs.

1.3 Website Analytics

When you browse our Site, we may collect: browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and device type.

1.4 Communication Data

If you contact us, we collect the information you provide in your communications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for: providing and maintaining the Services (processing API requests, account management, billing), improving the Services (performance monitoring, troubleshooting, feature development), security and fraud prevention (detecting abuse, unauthorized access, and policy violations), communication (sending service updates, billing notices, and responding to inquiries), and legal compliance (meeting regulatory obligations).

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT intentionally collect or store: personal data of your end users (your customers' customers), content of API requests beyond what is technically necessary for routing, sensitive personal information (health data, biometrics, etc.), or payment card details (handled by our PCI-compliant payment processor).

4. Data Storage & Transfer

4.1 Storage Locations: Your data is stored on servers located in the United States (New York).

4.2 Data Retention: Account data is retained for the duration of your account plus 90 days after termination. Usage logs are retained for up to 12 months. Billing records are retained for 7 years as required by Hong Kong law.

4.3 International Transfers: If you are located outside the United States, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

5.1 Third-Party Service Providers: We may share your information with payment processors (e.g., Stripe), cloud infrastructure providers, and analytics providers (anonymized data).

5.2 Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.

5.3 Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.

5.4 We Do NOT Sell Your Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest, access controls and authentication, regular security audits, and API key authentication. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to: access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data; data portability; and withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@gotapi.ai.

8. GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA):

8.1 Legal Basis: We process your data based on contract performance, legitimate interests (security, analytics, service improvement), and legal obligations.

8.2 Data Protection Officer: dpo@gotapi.ai

8.3 Complaints: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Cookies

9.1 What We Use: Essential cookies (required for Site functionality) and analytics cookies (to understand how visitors use our Site). We do NOT use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes.

9.2 Managing Cookies: You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the Site.
